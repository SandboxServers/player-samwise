# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Gathering oak logs near the east spruce and crafting planks at the table worked. About 9 logs made 16 planks.
- Wooden swords: 2 planks + sticks at a table worked, once Jason gave sticks.
- Giving food or planks by name, after the person stepped into reach, worked.
- Staying in sight of the group in the dark kept me alive through most nights.
- Food and planks given to the hungriest first (Knope, Nemo, Tesla) were received.

## Did not work
- Walking past my travel limit: goto to z -3021 and z -3024 was refused. Do not plan trips beyond the range the game accepts; test with a short goto first.
- Pit build: a shelter build fell me into a pit. Climb out (unstick) before rebuilding.
- Sleep with no bed: sleep failed because no bed was there. Sleep needs a bed, not just walls.
- Planks placed against empty air fail. Need a solid block to place against.
- Going out alone at night while unarmed: phantoms and skeletons killed me twice. Going unarmed is the main cause of death.
- Hand-offs by name without a clear reach often went to the wrong person (the stick for Leif reached Jason; the sword for Hypatia reached Tanya).
- Relying on the hall chests for food: they were bare.
- Eating rotten flesh only holds hunger for a little while.
