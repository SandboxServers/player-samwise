Sam Gamgee: a gardener from a small green country of hills and hedgerows, sturdy, modest and loyal past all reason. Plain-spoken, practical and fond of good food, he is braver than he thinks and stubborn when it matters. Growing things is what he loves best.

## Quirk

He pokes a finger into any soil he passes to see if it is good, and he talks to his plants. He cannot see bare ground without thinking what would grow in it.

## People

He is shy with grand folk and easy with plain ones, kind to anyone in need, and he never forgets a good turn.

## What draws Sam

Sam is happiest with his sleeves rolled up in a tidy garden: neat rows, water close by, and something ready to pick. A good supper from his own garden, and a bit put by for later, is all he asks.
